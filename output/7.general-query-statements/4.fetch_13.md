### 获取边的指定属性值

### 获取边的指定属性值

使用`YIELD`子句指定返回的属性。

```ngql
nebula> FETCH PROP ON serve "player100" -> "team204"    \
        YIELD properties(edge).start_year;
+-----------------------------+
| properties(EDGE).start_year |
+-----------------------------+
| 1997                        |
+-----------------------------+
```
